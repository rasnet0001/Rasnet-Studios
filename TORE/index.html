Story & Vision

A linear, highly cinematic narrative driven by fate and honor.

The Fate of a Bound Warrior

As the son of a nomadic chieftain, a highly skilled hunter and warrior, your life changes when you encounter and rescue a merchant and his daughter from the ruthless Templars. However, this act of righteousness pushes the boundaries of your clan's territory into an all-out war.

As you dig deeper, you realize this is not just about two refugees; the Templars are on the verge of executing a massive, catastrophic plot to wipe out millions of innocents. No choices, no alternate endings—just a powerful, dramatic tale of a hero fulfilling his destiny.

Two Worlds Collide

The unyielding justice of the Nomadic Code vs. Global Tyranny.

The Nomadic Clan

The Last Sanctuary for the Innocent

A deeply traditional, nature-bound people tightly tied to their ancient code ("Tore"). Armed with bows, arrows, and swords, their greatest strength is their unbreakable loyalty. By code, any innocent soul seeking shelter under their roof becomes a matter of absolute honor.

The Templars

The Global Oppression Machine

A merciless organization driven by pure malice and control. Operating from the shadows, they are executing a colossal plot to eliminate millions. With disciplined armies, heavy armor, and brutal efficiency, they crush anyone in their path.

Soundscape & Atmosphere

A mesmerizing musical blend of two clashing cultures.

The world of TORE comes alive not just through its striking stylized visuals, but through your ears. The nomadic spirit is captured through mystical ethnic string instruments (inspired by the kopuz and kemençe), clashing with the dark, heavy presence of grand church choirs and pipe organs representing the Templars.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the TORE project.

What does the Episodic release model mean? +
Instead of waiting years for a massive game to finish, we release self-contained storytelling episodes lasting roughly 3 hours each. This allows us to deliver high-quality content faster and adjust based on community feedback.
Why is there no open-world or choice system? +
TORE aims to tell a highly focused, linear, and emotionally heavy cinematic story. We wanted to eliminate unnecessary open-world fluff to keep players completely engaged in the intense narrative of the hero's destiny.
What are the target platforms? +
The game is being heavily optimized and developed primarily for PC (Steam / Epic Games Store).
