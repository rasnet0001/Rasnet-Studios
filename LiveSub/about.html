About LiveSub

LiveSub is a next-generation real-time subtitle translation platform designed to eliminate language barriers in digital content. In a world where entertainment, education, and communication are increasingly global, understanding content in different languages should not be a limitation. LiveSub was created with the vision of making every piece of content accessible, regardless of the language it was originally produced in.

By combining high-performance Optical Character Recognition (OCR) with advanced AI-powered translation systems, LiveSub allows users to capture subtitles directly from their screens and translate them instantly. This process happens in real time, meaning users can follow along with movies, series, livestreams, and games without interruption.

Our Mission

Our mission is to democratize access to information and entertainment by breaking down language barriers. We believe that everyone should have the ability to understand and enjoy content from around the world without needing to rely on pre-translated subtitles or manual translation tools.

LiveSub aims to empower users by providing a seamless, automated translation experience that works in the background while they focus on what matters most: enjoying content, learning new things, or communicating effectively.

Why LiveSub?

Traditional subtitle and translation tools often require manual input, pre-existing subtitle files, or switching between applications. LiveSub eliminates these limitations by working directly on your screen in real time.

• No need for subtitle files
• Works with any video or application
• Real-time processing with minimal delay
• Clean and customizable overlay interface
• Designed for both casual users and advanced users

How LiveSub Works

LiveSub operates through a powerful multi-step pipeline. First, the user selects a specific region of the screen where subtitles are displayed. The system then continuously scans this area using OCR technology to detect and extract text.

Once the text is captured, it is processed and sent to a translation engine. LiveSub supports multiple translation providers, allowing users to choose the one that best fits their needs in terms of speed and accuracy.

Finally, the translated text is displayed as an overlay on the screen. This overlay is fully customizable, allowing users to adjust font size, position, colors, and more.
