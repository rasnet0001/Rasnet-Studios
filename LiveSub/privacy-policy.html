Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how LiveSub collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our application and website. We are committed to ensuring that your privacy is protected and that your data is handled in a safe and transparent manner.

1. Information We Collect

LiveSub may collect limited information to provide and improve its services. This includes:

• Basic contact information (such as name and email) when you contact us
• Technical data such as device type, operating system, and usage behavior
• Error logs and diagnostic information for performance improvements

We do not collect sensitive personal information unless explicitly provided by the user.

2. Screen Content and OCR Processing

LiveSub uses OCR technology to capture text from your screen in real time. This process is performed locally on your device whenever possible.

We do not store or permanently save the content captured from your screen. The data is processed temporarily for translation purposes only.

Users are responsible for ensuring they have the right to capture and translate the content displayed on their screens.

3. How We Use Information

The information we collect is used to:

• Provide and maintain our services
• Improve performance and user experience
• Respond to user inquiries and support requests
• Develop new features and enhancements

4. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to enhance user experience. Cookies help us understand how users interact with the site.

You can choose to disable cookies through your browser settings.

5. Third-Party Services

LiveSub may integrate with third-party services such as translation providers. These services may process data according to their own privacy policies.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party services.

6. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

We retain collected data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy.

Temporary OCR and translation data is not stored permanently.

8. User Rights

Users have the right to:

• Request access to their data
• Request correction or deletion of their data
• Withdraw consent where applicable

9. Children's Privacy

LiveSub is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
