About the App

LiveSub is a Windows application that detects subtitles in games and other on-screen content in real time and translates them into the user's chosen language. It aims to make it easier for users—particularly those playing games that do not support their native language—to instantly understand on-screen dialogue and subtitles. The application detects subtitle text on the screen, translates it into the selected target language, and displays the translated text directly to the user. This enables the understanding of subtitles in various languages ​​without requiring additional language support to be added to the games or content.

Key features:

  • High accuracy and low latency
  • High performance and low resource usage.
  • User-friendly, modern interface design.
  • Advanced customization options.

Kullanım Kılavuzu

1. Setup: Download the app with a single click from the Microsoft Store. Installation will complete automatically.

2. Launch: Launch the application after the installation is complete.

3. Mode Select: Choose either "Faster" mode or "Pro" mode; the mode descriptions are as follows Faster: Operates with very low latency and incurs no additional cost, though it may make errors with subtitles that have noisy backgrounds. Pro: Operates slightly slower than "Faster"—though still with low latency—but is vastly superior to "Faster" in terms of subtitle accuracy.

4. Target Language: Select the language you want the subtitles translated into from the supported languages ​​and start the translation process.

5. API Key: Skip this step if you have selected "Faster" mode. Enter the API address you obtained from Google Cloud; if you do not know how to get the API, you can watch the video at this link.

6. Font Size: Please enter the font size you would like for your subtitles in the relevant section.

7. Start: Press the start button and enjoy.

Privacy

LiveSub respects your privacy. The application only processes the subtitle text necessary to provide its translation functionality.

Subtitle content may be sent to third-party translation services when required to translate it into your selected language. LiveSub does not intentionally collect or sell personal information, and subtitle data is not used for advertising or user profiling.

Some basic application settings may be stored locally on your device to improve your experience.

We aim to collect and process as little data as possible while providing LiveSub's core functionality.

If you have any questions about privacy or data processing, please contact Rasnet Studios through our official website.
