About the App

Stream Sultan is a simple, fast screen-sharing platform built on Peer-to-Peer (P2P) technology that allows users to share their screens and system audio with others in real-time directly through their browsers, without the need to install any software or applications. When a user starts a broadcast, they receive a unique link that can be shared to invite others to join; the service supports various quality options—such as 720p, 1080p, 2K, and 4K—as well as 30 FPS and 60 FPS settings. Stream Sultan requires no account creation or registration, does not record broadcasts, and establishes connections directly between users.

Key features:

  • High quality
  • Low latency
  • High level of confidentiality.
  • Advanced customization options.

User Manual

1. Start: Select your broadcast settings and start your broadcast.

2. Copy Link: Copy the link provided to you and share it with your friends who want to watch the broadcast.

3. Finish Enjoy high-quality, low-latency streaming.

Privacy

Stream Sultan values ​​user privacy and collects as little personal data as possible.

User screenshots, broadcast content, or shared system audio are not recorded by Stream Sultan.

Since screen sharing takes place via a P2P connection, content is transmitted directly between the broadcaster and the viewers.

Stream Sultan does not require account creation or the sharing of unnecessary personal information.

Except for technical data necessary for the service to function, user data is not sold or shared unnecessarily for advertising purposes.
