How it works

Three steps.
That's it.

You don't need an account, an application or a technical setup. Stream Sultan is built around a simple link-based experience.

01

Start a Stream

Open the Stream Sultan sharing page and choose the quality and frame rate you want to use.

02

Share Your Screen

Select the screen or window you want to share. Your browser handles the screen capture and connection setup.

03

Send the Link

Stream Sultan creates a unique viewing link. Send that link to your friends and they can connect to your active stream.

Built for direct streaming

Your screen goes
where it needs to go.

Stream Sultan uses WebRTC-based peer-to-peer connections for live screen delivery. The goal is straightforward: connect the broadcaster and viewers and let the stream flow in real time.

Peer-to-peer streaming

Once a viewer connects to an active stream, a WebRTC connection is established between the broadcaster and the viewer. Stream Sultan does not turn your live screen into a recorded video file.

This keeps the experience focused on what matters: real-time screen sharing without a traditional video hosting workflow.

Privacy focused by design

Live means live.

Stream Sultan is designed around temporary live sessions, not permanent video hosting.

🔒

Your stream isn't a recording.

Streams are designed to be transmitted live through peer-to-peer WebRTC connections rather than being saved as videos for later playback. There is no user account system required to start or watch a stream.

Frequently asked questions

Questions?
We've got answers.

A few things you may want to know before starting your first Stream Sultan session.

Do I need an account?

No. Stream Sultan does not require an account, registration or login.

Do viewers need an account?

No. A viewer can connect using the viewing link generated for the active stream.

What quality can I stream in?

The broadcaster can select 720p, 1080p or 4K, depending on what their setup and connection can support.

Can I stream at 60 FPS?

Yes. Stream Sultan supports both 30 FPS and 60 FPS stream settings.

Is system audio supported?

Yes. Screen sharing can include supported system audio along with the shared screen.

Can multiple people watch?

Yes. Multiple viewers can connect to an active stream using its viewing link.

Are streams recorded?

Stream Sultan is designed for live streaming. Streams are not saved as recordings for later playback.

What happens if the broadcaster disconnects?

The current session ends when the broadcaster disconnects. The broadcaster can start a new session and share the newly generated link.
