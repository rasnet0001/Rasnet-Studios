Privacy at Stream Sultan

Your screen is yours. Keep it that way.

Stream Sultan is designed for temporary, real-time screen sharing. No account is required, streams are not designed to become permanent recordings, and connections are established for active streaming sessions.

01. Privacy Overview

Stream Sultan is a browser-based screen sharing service built around a simple idea: start a live session, share the generated link and let other people watch.

Because the service does not use a traditional account system, there is no user profile that needs to be created before you can start streaming.

Stream Sultan also does not operate as a traditional video hosting platform. A stream is a live session rather than a video uploaded to a personal media library.

Important: Privacy on the web also depends on your browser, operating system, network provider and the people you choose to share your stream with. Stream Sultan cannot control what a viewer does with content they can see on their own device.

02. Accounts & Identity

Stream Sultan currently does not have a registration or login system.

This means you do not need to provide an account name, profile, password or other account details simply to start a stream.

No profile, no account dashboard

Stream Sultan does not provide user profiles, follower systems, public channels or a personal streaming dashboard.

The service is intentionally centered around individual streaming sessions rather than permanent identities.

03. What happens when you share your screen?

When you start a stream, your browser's screen sharing functionality asks you what you want to share.

Depending on your browser and operating system, this may include an entire display, a specific application window or another supported sharing source.

You make the selection.

Stream Sultan does not automatically decide which part of your screen should be shared. You interact with your browser's screen sharing interface and select the content you want to broadcast.

Screen and system audio

When supported by the browser and the selected capture source, system audio can be included alongside the screen stream.

05. Stream Links

When a broadcaster starts a session, Stream Sultan generates a unique viewing link for that session.

The link contains information required by the viewer page to connect to the corresponding active session.

Keep your stream link private when necessary. Anyone who obtains the active viewing link may potentially be able to attempt to connect to the stream. Only share it with people you trust.

What happens when a session ends?

The generated link is associated with the corresponding streaming session. If the broadcaster closes the session or needs to start again after a connection problem, a new session and connection can be created.

This means you should treat each generated stream link as a temporary access link rather than a permanent channel address.

06. Recording & Storage

Stream Sultan is designed for live screen sharing, not video recording or video-on-demand hosting.

The service does not provide a stream archive, recording library or replay page where previous streams can be browsed.

No permanent stream library

Once an active streaming session ends, Stream Sultan does not provide a built-in way for a viewer to return later and replay that stream.

One important limitation

Stream Sultan cannot prevent a viewer from independently recording or capturing content that is displayed on their own device. Screen sharing technology cannot guarantee that a recipient will not make their own copy of something they can see.

08. Browser Permissions

Screen sharing is a browser-controlled feature. Your browser determines when permission is requested and what capture sources are available.

Stream Sultan relies on these browser capabilities rather than secretly accessing your display.

You can stop sharing

You remain in control of the screen-sharing session through your browser and operating system. Stopping the capture ends the corresponding screen stream.

Browser compatibility matters

Available screen-sharing options, system-audio support, video constraints and WebRTC behavior can vary between browsers and operating systems.

09. Security

10. Third-Party Services & Infrastructure

Stream Sultan currently uses browser-based technologies and PeerJS for establishing the communication required by the application.

Browsers themselves are also independent software products with their own privacy policies, networking behavior and telemetry.

Your internet service provider, operating system, browser and network infrastructure may therefore process technical information independently of Stream Sultan.

Important: Third-party privacy practices are governed by those providers' own policies and are not controlled by Stream Sultan.

11. Changes to This Privacy Page

Stream Sultan may evolve over time. Features such as accounts, additional media sources, analytics, new infrastructure or other functionality could change how information is processed in the future.

If the service changes in a way that materially affects privacy, this page should be updated to describe the new behavior.
